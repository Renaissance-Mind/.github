# RenaissanceMind ｜ 认知宇宙

Renaissance Mind is building human-centered cognitive augmentation for the age of AI agents.

As AI agents become better at executing tasks, we care about a different question: **how can AI help people become better at learning, thinking, judging, and working with complex context?**

## What We Care About

- **Agent feedback**: helping people understand, manage, and review what agents do, instead of treating execution as a black box.
- **Cognitive augmentation**: exploring better ways for AI to support learning, brainstorming, reasoning, and decision-making.
- **Personal context**: building context layers that help AI understand a person's history, preferences, projects, and cognitive patterns.
